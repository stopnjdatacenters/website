# About

</div>
</section>

<section class="wrap section prose">

Stop NJ Data Centers is a nonpartisan group of residents working to protect our communities from irresponsible data center projects.

We don't oppose data centers or AI. We oppose building them in densely populated or environmentally sensitive areas, and anywhere without enforceable limits on noise, emissions, water and power costs, or a real voice for residents. The data center at 4405 Route 1 fails on both counts: it sits next to homes and near the Millstone River, and its draft air permit puts no limit on emergency generator use. See [what we're asking for](/act/#demands).

We collect the facts and ask our elected representatives where they stand, so residents have the information they need to make informed decisions.

We only publish information we can trace to a verifiable source, and we link to that source. If you spot an error, email us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and we'll correct it.
