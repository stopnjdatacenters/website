# About

</div>
</section>

<section class="wrap section prose">

Stop NJ Data Centers is a nonpartisan group of residents working to protect our communities from irresponsible data center projects. We collect the facts and ask our elected representatives where they stand, so residents have the information they need to make informed decisions.

We only publish information we can trace to a verifiable source, and we link to that source. If you spot an error, email us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) and we'll correct it.
